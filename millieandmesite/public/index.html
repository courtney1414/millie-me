A slower kind of love.

Repair, rent, rehome.

Millie & Me started with a simple idea: not everything needs to be new to be good. It just needs to be built to last — and worth passing along once it's proven itself.

We repair what's worth saving instead of writing it off — starting with sewing machines and alterations, with more on the way. We rent baby gear built to outlast a single family, instead of selling it once and forgetting it. And we rehome what's already had a good life — secondhand finds, well-loved books, cards worth trading, and small pieces of ephemera worth a second frame — quality that gets a second chance, not a rushed one.

Repair what you love. Rent what you need. Rehome what's ready for someone new. And stay for a slow cup of coffee while you're at it.
