millie & me
a family collective

Some things are better handed down.

Millie & Me believes the best things get passed around. A stroller that outlives one baby. A book that's been underlined by someone else. A card that changes hands across a table instead of a screen. We started with baby gear you rent instead of buy, and it grew from there — secondhand finds, a shelf of used books, cards worth trading, and coffee slow enough to make you stay and talk a while. Different things, same idea: nothing here was meant to be used once and thrown away — including the afternoon.
