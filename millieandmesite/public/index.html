Some things are better handed down.

Millie & Me started as one simple idea: the things our family needs shouldn't cost the earth, or end up in it after six months of use. What began as baby gear you rent instead of buy has grown into something bigger — a small family collective built one idea at a time, named for the good dog who's underfoot through all of it.
